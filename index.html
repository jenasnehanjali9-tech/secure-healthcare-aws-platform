Why it's built this way

Healthcare data can't be treated like ordinary data.

A patient record carries weight an ordinary row of data doesn't — it needs to stay private, stay accurate, and stay traceable. This project treats those three needs as infrastructure, not afterthoughts.

  • Encrypt everything at rest. Aurora storage and S3 objects are encrypted with customer-managed KMS keys, so the data is unreadable without the right key — even if the storage itself were exposed.
  • Limit who can see what. A clinician, an analyst, and an administrator each get exactly the access their job requires — nothing more.
  • Watch for drift. AWS Config checks the account continuously, catching things like a bucket accidentally made public before it becomes a problem.
  • Log every action. CloudTrail records who did what and when, across every region, with tamper-evident log validation.
  • Separate duties. The administrator who manages the infrastructure is explicitly denied access to read actual patient records.
  • Keep analytics honest. Analysts work from a de-identified copy of the data — never the raw patient-identifiable source.
